Happy Birthday ❤️

This is your 25th birthday, which means a quarter of a century. May you receive all the blessings you deserve, the love you deserve, and the care you deserve.

Shalni, you have seen so many struggles in these 25 years. But from here on, I hope you witness all the happiness, love, and care you deserve. Nee asa pattadhellam unakku kedaikkum. And finally, one thing: if this world ever feels too heavy, you just need to run towards me. If this whole world stands against you, I’ll stand by your side.

You have me — for the next 25 years, yes.
For the next 50 years, yes.

Nee inimae vaazha pora vazhkai sandhoshama irukkum. Kavala padadha. Edhayum yosichu varuthapadadha. Unakku edhu nadandhaalum, naan irundhen, irukken, iruppen.

Even though you don't like me, even though you hate me sometimes, edhayum nenachi kavala padadha. You’re going to shine like a star. ✨
